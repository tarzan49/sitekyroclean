Do not invent a different worker, equipment or brand mark. Reframe the photograph so the extraction nozzle touching the sofa and existing cleaned strip are the strong central visual; keep this action unobstructed. Design a highly legible premium mobile ad with intentional editorial art direction, deep forest green #071a12, white and restrained gold #D4AF37, clean Avenir Next-like sans serif. Bottom dark green gradient supports proof and a single vivid green WhatsApp call-to-action. No browser chrome, screenshot elements, navigation, unnecessary icons, sparkles, starbursts, badges or fake urgency. Keep all text comfortably within margins and readable at phone size. Typographic hierarchy must be strong, few elements, sophisticated rather than template clutter.
