# Imagem de desgaste das cadeiras

Imagem ilustrativa criada com a ferramenta integrada imagegen para o cartão «Desgaste e descoloração do tecido». Ficheiro: `src/assets/cadeira-tecido-desgastado.webp`. Otimizada para WebP com 1200px de largura.

## Prompt

Photorealistic editorial illustration for a professional upholstery cleaning website. One wide horizontal photograph 1536x1024, close-up three-quarter view of an upholstered dining chair seat and lower backrest in muted dark teal woven fabric. Clearly visible realistic wear from years of use: pale faded patches on contact areas, flattened fuzzy fibers, subtle pilling and abrasion along the front rounded seat edge. Intact upholstery, no holes, no tears, no wet stains, no spilled liquid, no dirt splashes. The original richer teal remains visible at sheltered seams, making discoloration easy to understand. Natural soft side daylight, neutral light room blurred background, a little wooden chair frame visible to establish unmistakably a dining chair. Compose the worn front seat edge centrally so it remains visible in a wide 2.6:1 website card crop. No people, no tools, no text, no logos, no before/after collage. Honest illustrative image of wear, not a promised cleaning result.
